Invoke one category, describe your goal and constraints, and the compatible host agent chooses relevant workflows. Existing stacks and brands stay part of the brief; an explicit choice takes priority.

Know what was checked.

Merge accounting, resource hashes, links and helper syntax can be checked locally. The evaluation corpus separates structural checks from human review and unexecuted browser, device or model-provider checks. A passing file check is not a guarantee of AI output quality.
