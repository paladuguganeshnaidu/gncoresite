What was improved

All thirty entrypoints gained original, domain-specific failure-prevention guidance. Existing workflows, resources and upstream notices are preserved. Final allocation follow-ups passed16 frozen and3 exploratory cases in each IDE; known-task follow-ups do not prove generalization.

What remains

Manual review is unblinded; Copilot Auto is not a pinned model; there are no repeated-trial estimates or no-skill controls. Cloud deployments, GPU training, hardware and production security were not certified. Raw answers, frozen criteria and failure logs remain available for review.
